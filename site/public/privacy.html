Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version. syscity.net is a static marketing page. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no contact form and no advertising. It sets no tracking cookies. The software you download runs on your own machine and does not report to us.

This policy covers syscity.net (this website) and the download links it serves. It does not cover third-party sites we link to, and it does not replace the terms of any model provider, chat platform or cloud service you choose to connect to your own Syscity installation.

1. What this website collects

Nothing that identifies you, directly

There is no account system, no comment form, no newsletter and no checkout on this site. We do not ask for a name, an email address or a payment detail, so we do not hold any.

Aggregate page-view counts

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It is cookieless and does not fingerprint visitors: it records the page URL, the referring page, the country (from the network, not from a stored profile), and coarse device/browser information. No identifier is stored in your browser, and the data is aggregated — we cannot pick out an individual visit.

Your language preference

If you switch the page to Chinese, the site stores one value in your browser's localStorage under the key syscity-lang, so the choice survives a reload. It never leaves your browser, and clearing site data removes it.

Server logs and bot protection

The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes every request in order to deliver it and to filter abusive traffic. To do that it may set a short-lived security cookie (__cf_bm) on automated or suspicious requests, and it keeps standard request logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) for a limited retention period. We do not combine those logs with anything else and do not use them to profile visitors.

2. What the Syscity software does

Syscity is local-first. Installed on your machine, it stores its memory, knowledge base and artifacts on your own disk. It talks to the network only where you configure it to:

  • Model providers you add (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, or a local Ollama) — those providers receive whatever you send them, under their own policies.
  • Chat channels and MCP servers you connect — likewise.
  • Syscity Cloud, if you sign in to it for hosted models, search or the marketplace. That service is governed by its own privacy policy, shown in the console.
  • Update checks, if enabled, which fetch release metadata from this domain.

The software does not include telemetry that sends your prompts, files or screen contents to us. Anything that leaves your machine goes to an endpoint you chose.

3. Cookies

This website sets no cookies of its own. Because of that there is no cookie banner — there is nothing to consent to. The only cookie that can appear is Cloudflare's short-lived bot-management cookie described above, which is strictly necessary for security and is not used for advertising or profiling.

4. Downloads

Installer and app downloads are served as static files from this domain. We do not embed per-download identifiers in the files. Your browser or operating system may still record the download locally.

5. Links to other sites

The page links to GitHub, Discord, the Apple App Store, Google Play and the Syscity Cloud console. Those sites have their own privacy policies; once you follow a link, this policy no longer applies.

6. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data tied to you, there is usually nothing to access, correct or delete. If you believe we do hold something about you, write to us and we will tell you what we have and remove it on request where we are able to.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially — for example if we ever add accounts or advertising — the “last updated” date above changes and the new policy is posted here before the change takes effect.
